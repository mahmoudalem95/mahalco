Critical Lane Volume

Signalized intersection phase calculator. Enter volumes and lane counts for each approach to get the critical volume using three phase methods: split approaches, protected left separation, and dual ring. Works for T‑intersections and multi‑lane approaches, handles shared through+right lanes and per‑approach free right turns. Calculation and schematic update in real time — no "Calculate" button needed.

Method Limitations

What this calculation does not cover. Critical volume is only one side of the equation, and there are cases the model does not represent:
  • Permitted left turns — here all lefts are protected. Left turns that filter through gaps require an equivalent‑through conversion factor.
  • Pedestrians — a minimum crossing time can constrain a phase even if vehicle volume is low.
  • Lost time — each phase has 3–5 seconds of lost time. Methods with more phases pay that penalty, offsetting some of the volume advantage.
  • Uneven lane utilization within a lane group, and heavy vehicles / grades (no pcu conversion here).
  • The design threshold is a user‑set parameter. The default (1500) is a common rule‑of‑thumb in CLV literature and is not a substitute for local agency guidelines.
