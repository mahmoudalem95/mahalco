Freeway Travel Time Reliability

Planning-level HCM-style reliability screening for a freeway facility. Enter observed or forecast travel-time statistics, demand, incident, weather, work-zone, calibration, and economic assumptions to calculate PTI, BI, TTI, MI, reliability LOS, scenarios, and annual user impacts.

Facility and travel-time statistics

Tideal = length ÷ ideal speed × 60. PTI = T95 ÷ Tideal; BI = (T95 − T50) ÷ T50; TTI = T50 ÷ Tideal; MI = T85 ÷ Tideal. Percentiles are constrained to non-decreasing order before calculation.
