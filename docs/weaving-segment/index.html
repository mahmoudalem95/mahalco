Weaving Segment Analysis

HCM Chapter 24-style screening for a freeway weaving section. Enter geometry, entering and exiting traffic, vehicle composition, calibration, ramp spacing, weather, work-zone, and safety observations to estimate weaving demand, capacity, speed, density, exact LOS, ramp warrants, and improvement opportunities.

GEOMETRY, TRAFFIC CONTROL, AND CALIBRATION

Adjusted FFS = (base FFS − lane-width adjustment) × driver factor + calibration. Capacity uses FFS, total/weaving lanes, facility/configuration type, length factor, and calibration factor.

TRAFFIC VOLUMES AND HEAVY VEHICLES

Vw = on-ramp + off-ramp; Vnw = through + other. fHV = 1 / [1 + PT(ET−1) + PR(ER−1)]; PCE flow = volume / (PHF × fHV).

RAMP SPACING, ADJACENCY, AND WARRANT INPUTS

Ramp-spacing warrant screens each spacing against the user-defined desired spacing and flags adjacent ramps when they reduce decision distance or create a compound weaving section.
