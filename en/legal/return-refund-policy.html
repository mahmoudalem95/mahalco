Return & Refund Policy — Defect Only

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Thank you for using Mahalco. Mahalco provides online engineering calculation tools, software services, API access, subscriptions, and related digital services. Because these products are primarily digital, access may be provided immediately after purchase.

1. Defect-Only Refund Policy

Mahalco generally does not offer refunds for change of mind, unused time, dissatisfaction with a result, failure to review product documentation before purchase, or a decision not to use the service.

Refunds or other remedies are considered where a purchased Mahalco digital service is affected by a material technical defect attributable to Mahalco that prevents the customer from reasonably accessing or using the purchased service and the defect cannot be reasonably resolved.

Important: This defect-only policy applies subject to any mandatory consumer, cancellation, refund, digital-content, subscription, or other rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited in the customer's jurisdiction.

2. What Qualifies as a Defect

A qualifying defect may include, for example:

  • paid access is not provided after successful payment and reasonable troubleshooting;
  • an API key or subscription entitlement issued by Mahalco does not function as intended;
  • a material server-side malfunction prevents reasonable use of the purchased service;
  • a purchased digital file is technically corrupted or cannot reasonably be accessed; or
  • another material technical failure directly attributable to Mahalco prevents the purchased service from being reasonably used.

3. What Does Not Normally Qualify

The following generally do not constitute a refundable defect:

  • change of mind after purchase;
  • failure to use the service;
  • failure to read product descriptions, documentation, pricing, or usage limits before purchase;
  • results that differ from a customer's expectation where the service itself is operating as described;
  • engineering, planning, design, or professional decisions based on the service output;
  • customer-side internet, device, browser, network, software, account, or configuration problems;
  • incorrect customer input or misuse of the service;
  • third-party outages or services outside Mahalco's reasonable control; or
  • unused subscription time following cancellation, except where a refund is required by applicable law or expressly approved under this policy.

4. API Services and Subscriptions

API fees, subscription fees, and usage charges are generally non-refundable once the service has been successfully accessed or used, except in the case of a qualifying material defect or where applicable law requires another remedy.

If an API or subscription is materially defective, Mahalco may first attempt to restore the service, correct the account, replace credentials, or otherwise provide functional access.

5. First Remedy: Technical Resolution

Before approving a monetary refund, Mahalco may reasonably attempt to resolve a verified defect. Depending on the circumstances, Mahalco may:

  • fix the technical problem;
  • restore or activate access;
  • replace an API key or access credential;
  • provide replacement access;
  • extend the applicable access period; or
  • provide another reasonable technical remedy.

If the material defect cannot reasonably be resolved or the purchased service cannot reasonably be provided, Mahalco may issue an appropriate refund.

6. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges

Verified duplicate charges or payment-processing errors may be refunded independently of the defect-only rule.

7. Refund Request Period

Customers should report a suspected defect as soon as reasonably possible after discovering it and while the issue can still be investigated. For digital services, prompt reporting helps Mahalco distinguish a service defect from customer-side or temporary technical issues.

8. Information Required for a Defect Claim

To investigate a defect, please provide:

  • your name;
  • the email address associated with the purchase;
  • the purchase date or order information;
  • the product, API, or subscription involved;
  • a description of the technical defect;
  • the approximate date and time the problem occurred; and
  • relevant screenshots, error messages, request IDs, or other non-confidential evidence, where available.

Do not email passwords, API keys, payment-card numbers, or other confidential credentials.

9. Refund Method and Timing

When a refund is approved, it will generally be returned through the original payment method. The time required for the refund to appear may depend on the payment processor or financial institution.

10. Abuse, Fraud, and Unauthorized Sharing

Mahalco may investigate suspected fraudulent transactions, payment abuse, unauthorized account sharing, API-key sharing, or other misuse before issuing a refund. This does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

11. Professional and Engineering Outputs

Mahalco's tools and APIs provide software calculations, analyses, and other digital outputs. A customer disagreement with an engineering result, recommendation, or calculation does not by itself establish a technical defect if the service is functioning according to its stated design and documentation.

As stated in Mahalco's service terms, outputs require appropriate independent professional review before engineering, regulatory, construction, or other consequential reliance.

12. No Waiver of Mandatory Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict mandatory rights provided by applicable law. Where a customer's local law requires a cancellation, replacement, repair, refund, cooling-off right, or other remedy, that legal requirement will apply to the extent required.
