Shipping & Digital Delivery Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Thank you for using Mahalco. Mahalco provides online engineering calculation tools, software services, API access, subscriptions, and related digital services. This policy explains how delivery works for Mahalco products and services purchased through the website.

1. Digital Products — No Physical Shipping

Mahalco products covered by this policy are digital products and online services. They are delivered electronically through the Mahalco website, customer account, dashboard, API, email, or another electronic access method identified at checkout.

No physical package, parcel, document, equipment, or other tangible item is shipped. Therefore, there is no physical shipping carrier, shipping address requirement, customs shipment, or physical delivery charge for these products.

2. Worldwide Availability

Mahalco intends to make its digital services available to customers internationally. Because the services are delivered electronically, there is no geographic freight or parcel-delivery restriction.

Access remains subject to applicable laws, sanctions, export-control requirements, payment-provider restrictions, platform availability, and any country-specific restrictions that may legally apply to the customer or the service. Where mandatory local law provides a customer with rights that cannot be excluded, those rights continue to apply.

3. Delivery Time

Digital access is normally provided electronically after payment has been successfully processed and the order has been confirmed.

  • Instant or near-instant access: where the product is automatically fulfilled.
  • Manual activation: where an API key, account permission, or subscription must be activated manually.
  • Downloadable files: where a product includes a digital file, the customer receives access through the designated download or customer-account mechanism.

Any product-specific activation or access period shown at checkout takes precedence over the general delivery description above.

4. Shipping Cost

Digital delivery is free of shipping charges. No postage, courier, freight, or physical shipping fee is added to a digital-only order.

5. Taxes, VAT, Duties, and Government Charges

Shipping does not create customs or import charges because Mahalco digital products are not physically transported across borders. However, taxes or other mandatory charges may apply to a purchase according to the customer's country or jurisdiction and the payment/store configuration.

Any applicable tax shown at checkout is separate from the digital delivery process.

6. Customer Information

For digital delivery, customers should provide an accurate email address and any other information required to create or access their account. Customers are responsible for maintaining access to the email address and account used for their purchase.

7. Failed or Delayed Digital Delivery

If payment has been completed but digital access has not been received or activated within the expected period, please contact Mahalco. We may verify the transaction and:

  • restore or activate access;
  • re-send the applicable access information;
  • correct an account or activation problem; or
  • where the service cannot reasonably be provided because of a technical problem attributable to Mahalco, provide an appropriate remedy consistent with the Return & Refund Policy.

8. API and Subscription Services

API access and subscription services are delivered electronically. API credentials, account permissions, quotas, and subscription features may be activated after successful payment and verification.

Customers must protect their API keys and account credentials and must not share them with unauthorized third parties. Usage remains subject to the applicable subscription limits and service terms.

9. No Customs or Physical Returns

Since these products are digital, there are no physical shipments to return and no customs clearance process associated with ordinary digital delivery.

Refunds and billing remedies are governed by the Mahalco Return & Refund Policy, including its provisions concerning digital services, subscriptions, duplicate charges, technical problems, and API services.

10. Service Availability and Interruptions

Delivery means electronic provision of access and does not guarantee uninterrupted or continuous availability of the website, API, hosting provider, payment platform, internet infrastructure, or third-party services. Temporary interruptions may occur because of maintenance, security events, infrastructure failures, or circumstances outside Mahalco's reasonable control.

11. International and Legal Compliance

This policy is intended for international customers. Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or restrict mandatory consumer, tax, privacy, payment, or other legal rights that apply in the customer's jurisdiction.

If a mandatory law in the customer's country requires a different treatment of delivery, cancellation, refund, taxes, or consumer rights, that mandatory requirement will apply to the extent required by law.
