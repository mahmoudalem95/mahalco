Engineering notes

  1. Generated dimensions are project-development parameters, not a universal agency standard.
  2. Accessibility screening uses a 1.22 m continuous clear pedestrian-route check. Passing-space arrangements must be confirmed for the governing standard and project conditions.
  3. The geometric framework is intended to be checked against the current adopted AASHTO Green Book edition, agency criteria, and project context. AASHTO does not impose one universal urban typical section.
  4. Construction depths, kerb reveal, median upstand and footway crossfall drive the drawn geometry only. They are illustrative defaults and carry no pavement-design intent.
  5. Vertical relief is exaggerated so kerbs and crossfalls read at plotting size. People, vehicles and trees are drawn at true horizontal scale and are not exaggerated.
  6. Confirm drainage, kerb and channel, pavement structure, utilities, ROW, cycle treatment, parking, transit, landscaping and local standards before issuing drawings.
