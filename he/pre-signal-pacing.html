Pre-Signal Pacing at Yosftal – Rav Nisenbaum

The real junction: divided Yosftal Street running east↔west (labelled NE = the east approach, W = the west approach below) meeting Rav Nisenbaum Street from the south (S), with red-marked median bus lanes that turn through the junction, the channelised free right behind the triangle island, and every crosswalk. Left: today's signal. Drivers queue at the stop line and the free right looks for gaps. Right: pre-signal pacing, upstream pre-signals with platoon speed guidance in the spirit of GLOSA and pacemaker lights. Pre-lights on the last 250 m set the platoon head's acceleration so it reaches the stop line as the green opens, already moving. Everyone behind forms up by ordinary GM car-following. The stop-line signal still guards the conflict zone. Both sides get identical arrivals, now drawn from the real manual traffic count (survey #2526, Monday 03/03/2025) instead of an assumed uniform demand.

What was read from the photo

Geometry (lane count, turn radii, the triangle island, the crosswalks) traced from an aerial photo of Junction #2891, Yosftal St. × Rav Nisenbaum St., Bat Yam. The turning percentages and bus/BRT split below are still photo-based, not counted.

  • NE approach (Yosftal St., east side): 4 lanes, two lefts to Rav Nisenbaum (the ↙ arrow and the red bus sit in the inner lanes) and two throughs continuing west on Yosftal. NE exit: 3 lanes, joined by the free right.
  • W approach (Yosftal St., west side): 4 lanes, three through (continuing east on Yosftal) and a right turn beside the painted chevron. W exit: 3 lanes.
  • S approach (Rav Nisenbaum St.): 2 lanes turning left onto Yosftal westbound; the free right S→NE runs behind the triangle island and gives way to W through traffic. S exit: 3 lanes.
  • Median bus lanes on the W and S legs, red where they meet the junction, turning W↔S through the centre. The phase plan (which movements share a green) is read from the image, not from the operating signal plan. Edit PLAN at the top of the script to correct it.

What was read from the counts

Manual classified turning-movement count, Sheeran Surveys, survey #2526, junction #2891 (“יוספטל - ניסנבאום”), counted Monday 03/03/2025, 06:00-20:00, processed with Maatz report producer v0.92.4. The q (car, veh/h) and bus (veh/h) fields on every car lane in LANES now come from this count's PM peak hour (17:00-18:00); the “Demand” slider scales them together. Observed queue lengths (vehicles stopped at the end of red, from the survey's manual queue-length log) are shown for comparison with what each simulation produces on its own.

  • NE / Yosftal east (4 lanes): counted 542.6 veh/h turning left to Rav Nisenbaum + 146.8 veh/h through to Yosftal west, plus 36 buses/h. Observed queue at end of red: AM peak avg ≈4-9 veh/lane (max 18), PM peak avg ≈7-14 veh/lane (max 18) — this arm is the more congested one in the evening.
  • W / Yosftal west (3 through lanes + 1 free-right lane): counted 282.6 veh/h through to Yosftal east + 589.4 veh/h on the free right to Rav Nisenbaum, plus 30 buses/h. Observed queue on the 3 signalled through lanes: AM peak avg ≈4-10 veh/lane (max 24), PM peak avg ≈3-8 veh/lane (max 10).
  • S / Rav Nisenbaum (2 lanes + 1 free-right lane): counted 617 veh/h turning left to Yosftal west + 626.2 veh/h on the free right to Yosftal east, plus 28 buses/h. Observed queue: AM peak avg ≈2 veh/lane, PM peak avg ≈2 veh/lane (max 3) — short queues, consistent with the free right absorbing most of this arm's traffic.
  • A second, related count (survey #109, junction #24061605, “יוספטל / יוספטל”) covers a nearby self-intersecting loop of Yosftal St. and was not used here — this page models junction #2891 only.
