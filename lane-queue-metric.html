Lane and Queue Analysis (Metric)

A quick operational tool to estimate queue lengths, lane group capacity and LOS using Metric units.

OUTPUT (METRIC)

QUEUE LENGTH: -- veh

QUEUE METERS: -- approx m

V/C RATIO: --

LOS: --

Metric Value Interpretation
Capacity -- lane-group capacity estimate
95th percentile queue -- 95th percentile design queue
Queue storage -- AASHTO design storage
Method -- Intersection lane-group method
