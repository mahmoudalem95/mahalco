Engineering notes

  1. Generated dimensions are project-development parameters, not a universal agency standard.
  2. Lane, shoulder and clear-zone widths should be checked against the current adopted AASHTO Green Book edition (or governing agency criteria) for the selected functional class, design speed and terrain.
  3. Clear-zone width is a function of design speed, traffic volume, and foreslope/backslope rate — the value here is an input, not a calculated result. Confirm against the applicable clear-zone table.
  4. The tool screens whether the foreslope rate shown is "traversable" (flatter than or equal to 1:4) for clear-zone purposes; slopes steeper than 1:3 are flagged as generally non-recoverable and a candidate for barrier.
  5. Guardrail is shown only as a schematic screening flag, not a placement design — actual warrants depend on embankment height, slope, fixed objects, and agency barrier-warrant criteria.
  6. Construction depths, ditch geometry and slope rates drive the drawn geometry only. They are illustrative defaults and carry no pavement or drainage design intent.
  7. Vertical relief is exaggerated so shoulders, ditches and slopes read at plotting size. Vehicles and signage are drawn at true horizontal scale and are not exaggerated.
  8. Confirm drainage design, pavement structure, superelevation, sight distance, right-of-way, utilities and local standards before issuing drawings.
